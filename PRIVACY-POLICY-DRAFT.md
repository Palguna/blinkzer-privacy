# Blinkzer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Blinkzer (package `com.foursagez.blinkzer`) is a creature recognition puzzle game developed by 4Sagez Studio and published by Maclepro Inc. This policy explains how the game handles information. Features can vary by platform and build.

## Information stored on your device

Blinkzer stores game progress, puzzle results, collection records, scores, virtual currency, rewards, play statistics, preferences, ad counters, and local purchase or entitlement records on your device. These records let the game resume, award rewards, and remember your settings. The game does not require a separate Blinkzer account. The reviewed game code does not request your name, email address, contacts, precise location, camera, or microphone for gameplay.

## Advertising and privacy choices

Mobile builds include Google AdMob for interstitial and optional rewarded advertisements. When the advertising SDK runs, Google may collect IP addresses (which can indicate approximate location), device or account identifiers, ad and app interactions, and diagnostic information. Google uses this information to deliver and measure advertising, analyze performance, and prevent fraud. The information processed depends on your device, consent choices, and SDK configuration. Test advertisements do not mean that no network data is processed.

Blinkzer uses Google's consent interface where available. You can revisit your choices through Settings > Privacy options when that option is offered. You can also manage advertising identifiers through your device settings. See [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's advertising information](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads).

## Children and Kids Mode

Blinkzer includes a Kids Mode setting that disables interstitial and rewarded advertisements and stops the game's advertising backend. Kids Mode is off by default in the current development build. The current build does not automatically verify a player's age. A parent or guardian should enable Kids Mode before allowing a child to play. Kids Mode does not disable all internet features: image downloads and an enabled platform cloud-save service can still communicate with their providers. It also cannot undo information already processed before it was enabled.

Parents or guardians can contact us about a child's information using the contact details below. Do not include a child's personal information in a public issue or comment.

## Cloud saves

In builds where cloud saving is configured and you are signed in, game progress and preferences may be synchronized using Google Play Games on Android or Apple's Game Center saved-game service on iOS. The platform associates the saved game with your platform account. Blinkzer does not receive your platform password. Purchase and entitlement records are excluded from the game's cloud profile. If the cloud service is unavailable or you are not signed in, the game can retain progress locally.

These services are governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/). Cloud saving is not active in every build.

## Creature images and external links

Some creature images are bundled with the game. Others are downloaded from Wikimedia Commons and cached on your device. The image host receives network request information, such as your IP address and requested image, when a download occurs. See the [Wikimedia Privacy Policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy). Opening an external link subjects that visit to the destination's privacy practices.

## Purchases

The current development implementation simulates purchases locally and has no live billing integration. It does not collect payment-card information. If live platform billing is introduced, this policy will be updated to describe that implementation before release.

## Retention and deletion

Local progress stays on your device until reset or removed. Settings > Reset progress resets levels, currency, collection, and statistics while preserving purchase and entitlement records. Where cloud sync is active, the game attempts to synchronize the reset profile; this is not a request to erase all records held by Google or Apple. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling removes local app data, subject to your device's backup and restore settings. Cloud or device backups may restore data later.

Use your platform account's data controls to manage platform-held game data, and contact us if you need help. Advertising and hosting providers apply their own retention rules and privacy controls. We do not set a single retention period for information held by those independent providers.

## Security and international processing

Local saves rely on your device's security. The game's configured image-download URLs use HTTPS, and Google's Mobile Ads SDK uses encryption in transit for collected data. No storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security. Service providers may process information in countries other than your own under their respective privacy policies.

## Your choices and requests

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict the use of personal information, or withdraw consent. Use the in-game and platform controls described above, or contact us for help with a request. We may need enough information to understand and verify a request; please do not send passwords or payment-card details.

## Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Material changes may also be communicated in the game or store listing.
