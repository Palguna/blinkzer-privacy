Blinkzer Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Blinkzer (Android package and iOS bundle ID com.foursagez.blinkzer) is a creature recognition puzzle game developed by 4Sagez Studio and published by MACLEPRO INC ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have.

Blinkzer has no Blinkzer account and we do not run a server that receives your game data. We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise location, camera or microphone data. The game contains no chat or user-generated content, and no analytics, crash-reporting, attribution or social SDKs of its own.

1. Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress, puzzle results, collection, scores, in-game currency, rewards, play statistics, settings, ad frequency counters and a local record of your purchases on your device, so it can resume where you left off and remember your choices. This data stays on your device unless cloud save (section 4) copies it to your own platform account.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Blinkzer shows interstitial ads and optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. When ads are requested, Google may collect your device's IP address (from which approximate location can be inferred), the advertising identifier (the Android Advertising ID; on iOS, Blinkzer does not ask for tracking permission, so Apple's IDFA is not available to it), other device identifiers, ad interactions, and diagnostic and performance information. Google uses this to serve, measure and personalise ads, for analytics and to prevent fraud. We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you.

Where required (for example in the EEA, the UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws), the game asks for your consent through Google's consent tool before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy options. On iOS, Blinkzer does not show Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites; ads on iOS are served without your IDFA. On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings. Buying Remove Ads stops interstitial ads.

See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

3. In-app purchases

Blinkzer offers optional in-app purchases. Payments are processed entirely by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iOS). We never receive your card number, billing address or payment details. The store tells the game which products you own, and the game keeps a local record of that so your purchases work offline. Restore Purchases in the game asks the store to confirm your purchases again, for example on a new device. Purchase records are handled under Google's Privacy Policy and Apple's Privacy Policy.

4. Cloud save

Blinkzer can keep a copy of your game progress in your own platform account, so it survives a lost phone and follows you to a new device. We cannot read this copy. Purchases are never included in it; they are restored from the store instead.

Android, through Google Play Games. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional. When you sign in, the game uses Play Games Saved Games, which stores your progress in the hidden app-data area of your Google Drive, readable only by Blinkzer. Google shares a player identifier and your Play Games profile name with the game to link the save to your account; the game uses them only for that and does not send them to us. The game does not receive your email address or password.

iOS and iPadOS, through Game Center and iCloud. Game Center signs in automatically with the Apple ID on your device. Your progress is stored as a Game Center saved game in your own iCloud account, readable only by Blinkzer. The game does not receive your Apple ID email address or password.

These copies are held by Google and Apple under their privacy policies. If you are not signed in, or the service is unavailable, the game keeps your progress on the device only.

5. Creature images

Some creature images ship with the game; others are downloaded over HTTPS from Wikimedia Commons and cached on your device. When an image is downloaded, Wikimedia receives standard request information such as your IP address and the image requested. See the Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy. Links that open outside the game are governed by the destination's own privacy practices.

7. Children

Blinkzer includes Kids Mode (Settings). With Kids Mode on, the game shows no ads and does not start the advertising SDK, so no advertising data is collected. A parent or guardian should turn on Kids Mode before a child plays. Kids Mode does not turn off image downloads or platform cloud save. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's information has been processed, contact us below and we will help.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data stays on your device until you use Settings > Reset progress, clear the app's data, or uninstall the game (device backups may restore it). Reset progress keeps your purchase records. To delete a cloud copy, remove the game's saved data from your account: on Android in the Google Play Games app or your Google Account's Drive app-data settings, and on iOS in Settings > [your name] > iCloud > Manage Storage. Google, Apple and Wikimedia keep data under their own retention policies.

9. Security and international transfers

Network connections made by the game use encryption in transit (HTTPS). Local saves are protected by your device's security. Our service providers may process information in countries other than your own under their privacy policies.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict use of your personal information, or to withdraw consent. Because we hold no personal data on our own servers, most of these rights are exercised through the in-game controls above and your Google or Apple account settings. We are happy to help with any request; please do not send passwords or payment details.

11. Changes

We will update this page when our practices change and revise the date above. Significant changes may also be announced in the game or on its store page.
